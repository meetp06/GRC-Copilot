# Human Resources Security Policy

**Owner:** Head of People · **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-24 · **Review cycle:** annual

## Background checks

All employees undergo a background check before their start date, covering criminal history
and employment verification, to the extent permitted by local law. Contractors with access
to production are subject to the same check. Results are reviewed by the Head of People and
retained for the duration of employment plus 12 months.

## Onboarding

New joiners sign a confidentiality agreement and an acceptable use policy before receiving
system access. Access is provisioned from a role template rather than by copying an existing
user's permissions. Onboarding is not complete until initial security training is finished.

## Security awareness training

Security awareness training is mandatory at hire and annually thereafter. Completion is
tracked and reported to the Head of Security. Simulated phishing exercises run quarterly;
employees who fail two consecutive exercises receive additional training.

## Acceptable use and confidentiality

Employees may access customer data only where there is a documented business need. All
employees are bound by confidentiality obligations that survive the end of employment.
Personal devices may not be used to store customer data.

## Disciplinary process

Violations of security policy are investigated by the Head of Security together with the
Head of People. Outcomes range from additional training to termination of employment,
proportionate to the severity and intent of the violation.
