# Data Privacy Policy

**Owner:** Head of Legal · **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-06 · **Review cycle:** annual

## Regulatory scope

We process customer personal data as a processor under the EU General Data Protection
Regulation and as a service provider under the California Consumer Privacy Act. A data
processing agreement incorporating the Standard Contractual Clauses is available and forms
part of the standard customer contract.

## Data subject requests

Requests to access, correct, export, or delete personal data are accepted through the
customer's designated contact. We acknowledge a request within 5 business days and complete
it within 30 days. Where the customer is the controller, we act only on their documented
instruction and do not respond to their end users directly.

## Data residency

Customer data is stored in the AWS eu-west-1 or us-east-1 region, selected by the customer
at provisioning. Data does not leave the selected region except for backups replicated to a
second region within the same jurisdiction. Support staff outside the region may access data
only through a session-recorded workflow.

## Personal data inventory

A record of processing activities is maintained listing the categories of personal data
processed, the purpose, the retention period, and the systems involved. The record is
reviewed annually and on any material change to the product.

## Privacy by design

New features that process personal data require a privacy review before release. A data
protection impact assessment is completed where the processing is high risk, including any
new use of automated decision-making or a new category of special category data.
