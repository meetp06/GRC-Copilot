# Vendor Management Policy

**Owner:** Head of Security · **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-11 · **Review cycle:** annual

## Vendor onboarding

Every vendor that will process customer data or connect to production is assessed before
contract signature. The assessment covers the vendor's security certifications, their
SOC 2 Type II report where one exists, their subprocessor list, and their breach
notification commitments. Vendors are tiered Critical, High, or Standard based on the
sensitivity of the data they handle.

## Subprocessors

A current list of subprocessors that process customer data is maintained and published.
Customers are notified at least 30 days before a new subprocessor is added, and may object
in writing during that window. Every subprocessor is bound by a written agreement with
data protection terms no less protective than our own customer terms.

## Contractual requirements

Contracts with Critical and High tier vendors require a data processing agreement, a
security incident notification commitment of 72 hours or less, a right to audit, and
deletion of our data within 30 days of contract termination.

## Ongoing monitoring

Critical tier vendors are reassessed annually. High tier every two years. Standard tier on
material change only. Reassessment covers renewed certifications, any publicly disclosed
breaches, and changes to the subprocessor list. Findings are tracked in the risk register.

## Vendor offboarding

On termination, vendor access to our systems is revoked within 4 hours. Written confirmation
of data deletion is requested and retained as evidence.
