# Incident Response Plan

**Owner:** Head of Security · **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-02 · **Review cycle:** annual

## Scope and severity

The plan covers confidentiality, integrity, and availability incidents affecting production
systems or customer data. Incidents are classified Sev1 through Sev4. Sev1 means confirmed
unauthorized access to customer data or a full production outage.

## Response process

Detection, triage, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident review. An incident
commander is assigned at declaration. Sev1 incidents page the on-call engineer within
5 minutes and require an incident commander within 15 minutes.

## Customer notification

Customers affected by a confirmed security incident involving their data are notified
without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirmation, via their designated security
contact. Notification includes what happened, what data was involved, and what actions we
have taken.

## Post-incident review

A blameless post-incident review is completed within 5 business days of resolution for all
Sev1 and Sev2 incidents. Action items are tracked to closure.

## Testing

The plan is tested at least annually through a tabletop exercise. The most recent exercise
was completed in March 2026.
